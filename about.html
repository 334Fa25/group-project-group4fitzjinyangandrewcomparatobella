Our Story

Founded in 2018 by passionate horticulturist Jamie Rivers, BrightSprout Landscaping was born from a vision: to create outdoor spaces that are not only stunning but also environmentally responsible. What started as a small design studio is now growing into a dedicated team of landscaping professionals, each bringing unique skills and creativity to every project.

At BrightSprout, we believe that a landscape should not only look beautiful—it should work in harmony with the environment. Our designs focus on creating gardens with sustainable practices, or outdoor spaces that thrive year-round, support local ecosystems, and reflect the unique character of each property. Whether it's a cozy backyard garden, a sprawling lawn, or a vibrant commercial property, our designs are tailored to enhance the beauty and functionality of every space we touch.

Jamie Rivers | Owner & Designer

"I believe a garden should be both beautiful and kind to the planet."

Jamie’s expertise in horticulture and sustainable design guides every project. With a keen eye for detail and a deep respect for the natural environment, Jamie ensures the landscape is not only visually stunning but ecologically responsible.
